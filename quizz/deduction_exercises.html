Notation
  • ¬ not · ∧ and · ∨ or · ⇒ implies.
  • Resolution (Robinson): to prove a goal, add its negation, convert everything to CNF, split into clauses, then resolve until the empty clause ⊥ appears.
  • Chaining works on Horn rules (IF–THEN) + facts.
